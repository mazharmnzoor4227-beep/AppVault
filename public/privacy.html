Privacy

Privacy policy

AppVault is designed to collect only what is needed to operate the site.

Last updated: 23 September 2026.

Browsing and downloads

You do not need an account to browse or download. We keep an aggregate count of download starts for each app; a count does not prove an installation. Our application does not attach a name or email address to a download.

Messages you send

The contact form stores your name, email, message and submission time in our Cloudflare-hosted database. The site administrator can read these to handle your request. Messages remain until the administrator deletes them. To request access or deletion, use the Contact page and identify the email you used. Please do not include passwords, payment details or identity documents.

Security and hosting

Cloudflare hosts this website and its files and may process IP addresses, device information and request logs to deliver and protect the service. Our abuse protection stores a temporary, hashed request identifier and attempt count. Expired records are cleaned up during subsequent protected requests; raw IP addresses are not stored in that table. Cloudflare may process data in countries other than your own.

Admin access

The private publisher console uses a Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite session cookie lasting up to 12 hours. Signing out clears the cookie. The console may remember the publisher's last developer name in local browser storage. Clearing site data removes that preference. Visitors do not need this cookie or preference.

Tracking and third-party apps

This version does not integrate advertising networks or third-party analytics. Any future integration will require an updated notice and appropriate controls. Downloaded apps are separate products; their permissions, data collection and privacy terms may differ from this website. Review them before installing.

Questions or requests

Contact AppVault about privacy, a listing, or your submitted information. This notice describes the current implementation; it is not a certification of legal compliance.

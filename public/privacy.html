Privacy policy

AppVault is designed to collect only what is needed to operate the site.

When you download an app, AppVault may record a basic download event so aggregate download counts can be shown. The site does not need your name or email for downloading. If you use the contact form, the name, email address and message you submit are stored so the site owner can review and respond to your request. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard technical information such as IP addresses, request logs and device information as part of operating and securing the service. If advertising or analytics services are enabled later, this policy should be updated to identify those services and any cookies or identifiers they use. Visitors should also be given any consent controls required by applicable law and the advertising provider. Do not place passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in the contact form.

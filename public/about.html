About AppVault

A straightforward way to publish and discover Android apps.

AppVault is an independent Android app library built around dedicated app pages, useful metadata, clear navigation and direct downloads.

Clear by design

Every app can have its own page, category, screenshots, version details and changelog so visitors can understand a listing quickly.

Creator managed

The private dashboard keeps publishing, categories, APK files, screenshots and download counts in one place.

Responsible distribution

Only apps and media that AppVault has permission to distribute should be published. Copyright and ownership remain with their respective holders.

About AppVault

A cleaner way to publish and discover apps.

AppVault is designed as an independent Android app library with dedicated app pages, useful metadata, simple navigation and direct downloads. The goal is to make every listing easy to understand instead of turning the whole website into one crowded feed.

Clear by design

Every app gets its own page, category, screenshots, version details and changelog so visitors can make informed choices.

Creator managed

The private dashboard keeps publishing, categories, APK files, screenshots and download counts in one place.

Responsible distribution

Only apps and media that AppVault has permission to distribute should be published. Copyright and ownership remain with their respective holders.
